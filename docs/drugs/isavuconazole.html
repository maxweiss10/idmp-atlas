Antimicrobial dosing

Isavuconazole

ID-R · UCSFID-R · ZSFGIV → PO

Restriction

UCSF: Restricted to ID or Antimicrobial Stewardship except:​

IV formulation: Inability to tolerate or absorb enteral medications AND one of the following:

PO capsule: One of the following:

1) Documented or suspected serious fungal infection in a patient with prolonged QTc interval​

2) Prophylaxis against fungal infections in patients on the HBC service or lung transplant service with prolonged QTc interval or concurrent QTc prolonging medications.

Adult dosing, non-dialysis

IndicationDoseNotes
All Indications372 mg IV/PO Q8h x 6 doses (total of 48h), then 372 mg Q24hNo renal dose adjustment

Dosing in intermittent HD and CRRT

Intermittent HD assumes high-flux hemodialysis. CRRT assumes CVVHD with ultrafiltration rate 2 L/h and residual native GFR < 10 mL/min (source assumptions).

IndicationDoseNotes
All Indications372 mg IV/PO Q8h x 6 doses (48h), then 372 mg IV/PO Q24hNo dose adjustment in hemodialysis

Dialysis notes

Intermittent HD assumes high-flux hemodialysis. CRRT assumes CVVHD with ultrafiltration rate 2L/h and residual native GFR.

Notes

General Considerations

- For assistance with therapeutic drug monitoring (TDM), see Azole TDM Reference Document.
- Review medications for potential drug interactions.
- 372 mg of isavuconazonium = 200 mg of isavuconazole.
