Antimicrobial dosing

Ceftriaxone

Adult dosing, non-dialysis

IndicationDoseNotes
Standard dosing*2 g IV q24hNo renal dose adjustment
Urinary tract infection1 g IV q24hNo renal dose adjustment
Enterococcal endocarditis (in combination with ampicillin) & meningitis2 g IV q12hNo renal dose adjustment

* In patients with obesity (defined as BMI >30 kg/m2), higher dosing may be indicated based on patient-specific factors, contact ID/ASP pharmacy for assistance.

Dosing in intermittent HD and CRRT

Intermittent HD assumes high-flux hemodialysis. CRRT assumes CVVHD with ultrafiltration rate 2 L/h and residual native GFR < 10 mL/min (source assumptions).

IndicationDoseNotes
Standard dosing*2 g IV q24h

No dose adjustment for hemodialysis

Urinary tract infection1 g IV q24h

No dose adjustment for hemodialysis

Enterococcal endocarditis (in combination with ampicillin) & meningitis2 g IV q12h

No dose adjustment for hemodialysis

* In patients with obesity (defined as BMI >30 kg/m2), higher dosing may be indicated based on patient-specific factors, contact ID/ASP pharmacy for assistance.

Where this drug is recommended

Adult empiric therapy

  • Abdominal Infections
  • Aspiration Pneumonia
  • Brain abscess
  • Cellulitis, abscess, cutaneous ulcer disease, necrotizing fasciitis
  • Chorioamnionitis and endometritis
  • Community Acquired Sepsis with Unknown Source
  • Community-Acquired Pneumonia
  • Epidural Abscess
  • Fever in a person who injects drugs
  • Meningitis
  • Pelvic Inflammatory Disease
  • Pneumonia, Hospital-acquired or Ventilator-associated
  • Septic Arthritis (No Hardware in Place)
  • Urinary Tract Infection (UTI)
  • Vertebral Osteomyelitis (No Hardware in Place)

Guidelines and pages

  • Necrotizing Soft Tissue Infection (NSTI)
