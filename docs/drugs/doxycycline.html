HD and CRRT

Intermittent HD assumes high-flux hemodialysis. CRRT assumes CVVHD with ultrafiltration rate 2 L/h and residual native GFR < 10 mL/min (source assumptions).

IndicationIntermittent HemodialysisContinuous Hemodialysis*
All indications100 mg IV/PO q12h (No adjustment for hemodialysis)100 mg IV/PO q12h (No adjustment for hemodialysis)

Where recommended

Adult empiric therapy

  • Acute Bacterial Exacerbation of Chronic Bronchitis (COPD)
  • Acute Bacterial Sinusitis
  • Chorioamnionitis and endometritis
  • Community-Acquired Pneumonia
  • Pelvic Inflammatory Disease
