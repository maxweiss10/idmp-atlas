Dosing in intermittent HD and CRRT

Intermittent HD assumes high-flux hemodialysis. CRRT assumes CVVHD with ultrafiltration rate 2 L/h and residual native GFR < 10 mL/min (source assumptions).

Where this drug is recommended

Adult empiric therapy

  • Abdominal Infections
  • Bites
  • Chronic Bacterial Sinusitis
  • Diarrhea
  • Fever in a person who injects drugs
  • Non-Vertebral Osteomyelitis
  • Outpatient treatment of low-risk neutropenic fever
  • Urinary Tract Infection (UTI)
