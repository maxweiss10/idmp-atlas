Dosing in intermittent HD and CRRT

Intermittent HD assumes high-flux hemodialysis. CRRT assumes CVVHD with ultrafiltration rate 2 L/h and residual native GFR < 10 mL/min (source assumptions).

Notes

Single-strength (SS) tablet contains 80 mg trimethoprim (TMP)

Double-strength (DS) tablet contains 160 mg trimethoprim (TMP)
