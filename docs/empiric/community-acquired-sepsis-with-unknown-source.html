Adult empiric therapy

Community Acquired Sepsis with Unknown Source

Community Acquired Sepsis with Unknown Source
Duration

Stop antibiotics at 48 hours if work-up does not reveal a source of infection

Duration should otherwise be tailored to clinical or microbiological source
Vancomycin IVsee page+Ceftriaxone2 g IV q24h+2 moreorPiperacillin/tazobactam (Zosyn)Loading dose = 4.5 g IV over 30 min x1, then 4.5 g IV infused over 4h every 8h (starting immediately after loading dose)orErtapenem1 g IV q24h

First choice

Vancomycin

PLUS one of:

Ceftriaxone

OR

Piperacillin/tazobactam

OR

Ertapenem (if known prior ESBL isolation)

Alternative

For severe beta-lactam allergy:

Vancomycin

PLUS

Metronidazole

PLUS

Aztreonam

Common pathogens

Enterobacteriaceae

S. aureus

Streptococci spp.

Comments

Follow empiric therapy based upon source or clinical syndrome, if known (e.g. pneumonia, UTI, etc.)
